A character who remembers

Dawnward notices what your character actually does in the game: the quests they turn in, the places they reach, the falls they survive. At a calm moment it asks a quiet question: how did that sit with them? Your answers, never the addon, shape who they become. Those answers turn into memories, story arcs and a private chronicle that you edit, approve and choose to share.

It works for every class, race and faction. A compassionate warlock, a ruthless paladin and a sceptical priest are all equally valid. Dawnward never decides who your character is from what they are.

How it works

  1. You play. Dawnward records only what the game verifiably reports: quests, levels, falls, places, dungeons, reputation tiers.
  2. A quiet question. Some moments become a question, and never in combat. A beginning always asks. Your homeland and the places, people and goals you care about weigh most. Ordinary travel is logged quietly.
  3. Your answer. Choose an answer or write your own. It may suggest a small change in personality, which applies only if you approve it.
  4. Your chronicle. A private draft records what happened and how they responded. Edit it, mark defining moments, and make public only what you choose.

Built to complement Total RP 3, not replace it

Total RP 3 is an essential part of the WoW roleplaying community. Dawnward Chronicles includes its own comprehensive character profile and storytelling tools, but it is not intended to replace Total RP 3's established public social roleplaying profile system. Dawnward helps players develop their characters through gameplay, while optional TRP3 integration lets them share approved public information with the broader roleplaying community.

  • Dawnward works without TRP3. It is an independent project, not affiliated with or endorsed by the Total RP 3 authors.
  • Writing to TRP3 is experimental and off by default. It only shares what you made public, backs up first, never writes while TRP3's editor is open, and can be rolled back.

Install a development build

Dawnward is not yet published on an addon site.

  1. Close World of Warcraft.
  2. Download the repository and copy the DawnwardChronicles folder to World of Warcraft\_classic_era_\Interface\AddOns\.
  3. Back up WTF\Account\<account>\SavedVariables\DawnwardChronicles*.lua before upgrading.
  4. Start the game, enable Dawnward Chronicles, and type /dawnward. The guide opens the first time.

Questions

Does Dawnward decide my character's personality?

No. It proposes small changes from your own answers, and nothing changes until you approve it. Class, race and faction never decide anything.

Will it post anything in chat or share my notes?

Never. Dawnward never sends chat, emotes or whispers. Everything starts private; only what you make public can be shared.

Why didn't a place ask me a question?

Questions are weighted and paced. Ordinary travel is logged quietly, and a cooldown keeps questions rare. A place tied to your story is far more likely to ask.

Do I need Total RP 3?

No. Dawnward works on its own. TRP3 sync is optional and experimental.

Does it work on WoW Forever?

Not yet verified. Compatibility is announced only after testing on a real Forever client.
