A character who remembers

Dawnward notices what your character actually does in the game (quests turned in, levels reached, falls survived) and, at a calm moment, asks a quiet question: how did that affect them? Your answers, never the addon, shape their personality over time and become entries in a private chronicle you can edit, approve and choose to share.

It works for every class, race and faction. A compassionate warlock, a ruthless paladin, a sceptical priest and an honourable rogue are all equally valid. Class never decides who your character is.

How it works

  1. 1

    You play

    Only what the game verifiably reports is recorded. Nothing is invented.

  2. 2

    A quiet question

    Shown at a calm moment, never in combat. Answer it, write your own, keep it for later or dismiss it.

  3. 3

    Your decision

    Proposes a one-step personality change, applied only when you approve. Always reversible.

  4. 4

    The chronicle

    A private journal draft: what happened, where, how they responded, what it may mean.

  5. 5

    Your choice to share

    Approve it, mark a defining moment, make it public, and optionally show it in Total RP 3.

Built to complement Total RP 3, not replace it

Dawnward Chronicles was created with one goal: to make roleplaying in World of Warcraft feel more alive. Total RP 3 is an essential part of the WoW roleplaying community, and Dawnward is not here to compete with it or replace what it already does so well.

While TRP3 gives you the tools to present your character to the world, Dawnward helps you discover who that character becomes along the way. Together, they can create a richer and more personal roleplaying experience.

  • TRP3 stays your public profile. Dawnward focuses on development, memories and storytelling.
  • An independent companion project, not an official or affiliated TRP3 extension.
  • Integration is optional, opt-in and experimental. It only edits a section Dawnward created, backs up first, and can be rolled back.
  • Only what you approve and make public can ever reach TRP3.

With thanks to the Total RP 3 team.
