# Dawnward Chronicles: product vision

## In one sentence
Dawnward Chronicles turns ordinary World of Warcraft play into a personal, evolving roleplay story. The game supplies the events, the player decides what they mean to their character, and Total RP 3 shows the result to other roleplayers as far as its supported integration allows.

```
Play WoW  →  something meaningful happens  →  "What does this mean to you?"  →  the character grows  →  your public profile reflects it
```

## What players experience
1. **Start in one of three ways** (all optional, and all can be revisited later):
   - **I already have a story:** import your Total RP 3 profile, or paste or write your biography. Dawnward keeps the original text exactly as written.
   - **Help me develop my character:** a short, skippable set of friendly questions that produces a starting foundation and an editable biography draft. The draft contains only what you said.
   - **Discover my story as I play:** skip setup entirely; your character takes shape through play.
2. **Play normally.** Dawnward quietly notices things that actually happened in the game: levels, quests, new places, dungeon victories, deaths, reputation and profession milestones.
3. **Story moments.** Now and then (never in combat, never mid-encounter, never more often than you allow), a small prompt asks how your character responds to something that just happened. It offers several plausible reactions, including "no lasting change", and you can always postpone or dismiss it.
4. **Your character grows.** Decisions accumulate. One choice never rewrites a personality, but a pattern of choices gradually shapes temperament, beliefs, motivations and relationships. Moods come and go; lasting changes become canon only when you agree.
5. **Your journal and biography keep up.** Every adventure, decision and note is in the journal. A structured biography (origins, relationships, defining experiences, recent developments, who they are now) is drafted from your approved facts and decisions for you to edit.
6. **Your public profile follows.** You choose which kinds of development may be public. Total RP 3's Currently text and titles update automatically through its supported API. The biography and personality traits are prepared for you to apply in one step, because Total RP 3 has no supported way for other addons to change them (see the TRP3 report).

## Principles
- **The player is the author.** Dawnward observes, asks and remembers. It never decides who the character is.
- **Events are facts; reactions are choices.** What happened in game is never confused with what it meant to the character.
- **Nothing becomes canon silently.** Suggestions and interpretations stay suggestions until approved. A story decision is the approval for its own direct, clearly labelled effects.
- **Private stays private.** Developments are private by default. Only categories you made public can be published, and secrets never are.
- **No stereotypes.** Class and race never decide personality.
- **No AI required.** A curated prompt library and transparent rules run entirely in the game client. There are no network calls and no companion apps.
- **It feels like WoW.** Small, themed, in-world prompts and the three existing sections (Journal, Character, Story). No dashboards and no stat minigame.
- **Honest about limits.** Dawnward never claims to understand free text or detect events that the game doesn't expose to addons.

## Who it is for
- Roleplayers with rich existing characters who want them to keep growing.
- Players who'd like a character but don't know where to start.
- Anyone who wants their adventures remembered as a story rather than a log.

## Targets
WoW Classic Era 1.15.9 first, with Total RP 3 3.4.1 as the audited integration. WoW Forever is a later, separate target. Dawnward works fully without Total RP 3.
