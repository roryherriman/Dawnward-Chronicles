# Audit (v0.10): Character profile and Character Showcase

Scope: `UI/CharacterView.lua` (profile, field editor, the new reader) and `UI/Showcase.lua` (model card, game and own-words panels).
Compared with: [character-profile.png](../mockups/character-profile.png) (`mockups/source/Character.dc.html`) and [proposed-character-showcase.png](../mockups/proposed-character-showcase.png) (`mockups/source/CharacterShowcase.dc.html`). The window shell, rail, header title and shared components (Widgets, Style, MainWindow) belong to the shell audit; they were not changed here.

Base: `origin/feat/v0.10-no-privacy` at `cc67ebf` (v0.9 layout with the privacy classification already removed).

**Nothing here has been checked in the game.** The L2 suite (WoW mock) proves wiring, sizes, positions, fits and colours; the look is the owner's in-game comparison (last column, and the steps at the end).

Privacy chips (Public / Private / Secret) shown in both mockups are deliberately left out (v0.10 rule); their space is re-flowed as described per row.

## 3. Shared components built locally (proposals for the shared files)

1. `CharacterView.DashedEdge(frame)`: a dashed 1 px edge (pooled textures, laid out only when the size changes). Candidate for `Style.Edge(frame, layer, inset, thickness, dashed)` and an "empty" state on `Widgets.Card`.
2. The reader (`CharacterView.AttachReader`): a full-section scrolling view with heading, hint, pooled paragraphs and cards, an action and "Back". Chronicle's full view is similar; a shared `Widgets.Reader` would serve both.
3. `Widgets.Tooltip` on a font string would fail in game (font strings take no scripts); the personality note therefore puts its tooltip on the panel. A guard in `Widgets.Tooltip` would prevent this elsewhere.
4. `MainWindow.HeadingFor`: the mockup's "Who she is" title (from the pronouns field) remains a shell decision.

## 5. In-game checks (compare with the named mockup)

1. `/reload`, `/dawnward`, Character, default window size, character-profile.png beside it.
2. First screen: six tiles (Name, Title, Pronouns, Race, Class, Age), then Summary and Motivation cards. Race and Class show the game's words in plain text with "from the game" under them; hover a tile for the tooltip. Edit Race, write your own, Save: it turns title white and the hint goes; Edit again, Clear: the game's comes back. `/dump DawnwardChroniclesDB` (or Export) shows no race until you wrote one.
3. Empty tiles (Age, Occupation, the Details group): a dashed outline with even dashes on all four sides and matching corners, no fill, "Not set" and "+ Add". Hover: fill appears. Click the tile (not the button): the editor opens.
4. Background & biography: import a story (Import story, paste several paragraphs, Save). The BIOGRAPHY card shows the first two paragraphs, "Pasted · N words" and "Read full story". Read it: the whole story, scrolls with the wheel and the slim bar; "Open in Import story" opens the window with the saved text; "Back to profile" returns where you were.
5. Right column: PERSONALITY with "changes need your approval" on two lines at the right of the eyebrow; six compact rows; unset axes show a hollow ring in the middle. No scroll bar in the right column. Add four or more family members: as many as fit, then "+N more"; it opens the whole list with Edit per member.
6. Family cards: name on one line, "Mother · living · notes…" on one line, cut with "…" when long; hover shows the full notes.
7. Showcase (proposed-character-showcase.png): turn buttons show the game's rotate arrows (check they point the way the model turns and do not look doubled inside the button frame); dimmed while the portrait shows. Wheel over the model zooms; over the right panels nothing moves, the camera does not zoom. "In your own words" starts with your title in Cinzel.
8. Window scale 0.7 and 1.4 (resize grip): nothing overlaps or clips; the "+N more" count is the same at both scales.
9. Themes Lightbound and Shadowbound: dashes, hints, hollow rings and the reader stay readable.
