# Audit: Character profile and Character Showcase (v0.8 visual fidelity)

Scope: `UI/CharacterView.lua` (profile, public preview, field editor) and `UI/Showcase.lua` (the Showcase view, the model card and its portrait fallback).
Compared with: [character-profile.png](../mockups/character-profile.png) (`mockups/source/Character.dc.html`) and [proposed-character-showcase.png](../mockups/proposed-character-showcase.png) (`mockups/source/CharacterShowcase.dc.html`). The rail, the header title and the shared components are covered in [shell-and-components.md](shell-and-components.md).

Base: `origin/claude/upbeat-mendel-ds1ct4` at `d7409bf`.

**Nothing here has been checked in the game.** The L2 suite (against the WoW mock) proves wiring, sizes, positions and colours. Whether it looks right is the owner's in-game comparison (steps at the end).

Priority: P1 is visible at a glance, P2 is visible on a closer look, P3 is a detail.

## 3. Files changed

- `DawnwardChronicles/UI/CharacterView.lua`: layout values from `Character.dc.html`; unboxed field groups; tiles, cards, empty fields, items and the Add line restyled; the local `paintVisibilityChip` removed in favour of the shared chip (`Widgets.VisibilityButton`), sized with `CharacterView.ChipWidth`; personality track, ringed knob, waiting marker and amber notes; header buttons at 36 and the showcase header; eyebrow per view; preview in the reading style; field editor at 36. New pure helpers `ChipWidth`, `ItemParts`. No saved-data change.
- `DawnwardChronicles/UI/Showcase.lua`: the model card's colours, stage, bar and zoom placement; `Showcase.Fallback` (framed round portrait); the right-hand panels' layout (`Showcase.PANEL`) and titles. `Showcase.Model`'s loading code (`TryModel`, `attempt`, retries, token, `setModelVisible`, events) is unchanged.
- Tests: `tests/spec/ui_character_layout_spec.lua` (new); `tests/spec/ui_character_profile_spec.lua` (two geometry/look assertions updated); `tests/spec/ui_shell_spec.lua` (panel title "FROM THE GAME"; three new Showcase layout tests).

## 4. Shared-component proposals (not changed here)

1. `Widgets.VisibilityButton`: use the chip text style (13 regular, as `Widgets.Chip`) and size its width to its word; screens now do the sizing themselves (`CharacterView.ChipWidth`).
2. `Widgets.Card`: an "empty" state (no fill, edge only) for unset fields; implemented locally as `paintFieldCard` in `CharacterView.lua`.
3. `MainWindow`: a section hook for its eyebrow (`def.eyebrowFor(section)`); the Character and Story sections set the eyebrow text directly after the shell's refresh.
4. A shared portrait-in-ring component: the rail and the Showcase fallback both build the ring, gap and well discs around `Showcase.Portrait`.
5. Bundle Alegreya Sans Italic (OFL) for the rail title and the "Not set" value.

## 5. Tests added (L2)

`ui_character_layout_spec.lua`: the mockup's grid (three tiles 10 apart, 268 column 20 away, inside the content area); chip and Edit fit every tile for every visibility, chip as wide as its word, 30 / 24 heights; a wrapped value stays clear of the controls and row tiles share a height; empty fields have no fill; card controls on the label row and text in the reading style; intro under the title and header buttons level with the close button, every header label fits; eyebrow per view and the showcase header; personality track, ring, knob centring at 0, 10 and 20, leaning pole colour, amber waiting note, marker and status, axes stacked inside the panel; family item parts and controls inside the card; every text at 13 or more; preview measure.
`ui_shell_spec.lua`: the model fills the stage (≥ 85 % of the card), 36 px turn buttons, the bar note on two lines, every mode chip label fits beside the zoom buttons; the portrait fallback is a fixed-size square portrait centred in a frame that fits the stage, with the right text per state and no zoom buttons; game keys fit their column, values at 16 in title white, and the three panels fit the content height with a long summary.

`python tools/check.py`: all checks ok, **896 passed, 0 failed** (882 before).

## 6. Manual in-game acceptance (compare with the named mockup)

1. `/reload`, `/dawnward`, Character. Put the window at its default size (Settings → General → Reset window size). Open character-profile.png beside it.
2. Header: CHARACTER eyebrow, "Profile", and the muted intro line directly under the title, not in the content. Four buttons on the right, as tall as the close button, none cut off.
3. Tiles: three per row on the dark window ground, 10 apart; gold Cinzel labels; values larger and whiter; each set field shows a pill-shaped chip (green Public, blue Private, red Secret) at the bottom left and "Edit" at the bottom right, neither touching. Point at a tile: lighter fill and edge; point at Edit: gold edge.
4. An empty field (e.g. Age): only an outline, "Not set" muted, "Add". Click a chip: it cycles and recolours, and the profile saves.
5. Summary and Motivation: full-width cards, label left, chip and a filled "Edit" on the same row, text larger with more space between lines.
6. Personality: poles small, the leaning pole bright gold; a thicker dark track; round gold knobs with a dark ring, centred on the track (square if masks are unavailable: report it). With a waiting change: amber note, an amber dot on the track where the value would go, amber status line and "Review in Story".
7. Family & relationships: each person as a card with the name, "Mother · living" and notes under it in muted text; Edit top right; chip under the text; quiet "Add" buttons.
8. Preview public view: eyebrow "CHARACTER · PUBLIC PREVIEW", text in comfortable lines, section titles in gold capitals, nothing private or secret.
9. Showcase (compare with proposed-character-showcase.png): eyebrow "CHARACTER · SHOWCASE", only "Profile fields" next to the close button.
10. Model card: darker than the panels with a dark gold edge; the live model fills its height; "Live model" gold chip top left; + / − at the top right over the model; at the bottom two square turn buttons and a two-line note. Drag, wheel and buttons still turn and zoom.
11. Click "Show portrait instead": the model disappears and a framed area appears in the middle of the card with a sharp round portrait (not stretched or blurry) in a gold ring, "Your portrait" and "You chose to show your portrait here…"; the chip says "Portrait (your choice)"; no zoom buttons. Click "Show the live model": the model is back. `/reload` while the portrait is chosen: it stays chosen.
12. Right side: FROM THE GAME with four evenly spaced rows and thin lines, class in its class colour; IN YOUR OWN WORDS with name, title, summary and the visibility chips; the portrait note below. Nothing overlaps or runs off the bottom with a long summary.
13. Switch theme (Lightbound, Shadowbound): every element follows the theme and stays readable. Shrink the window to its smallest size: text still readable.
