Summary

Raised above the Lion's Pride Inn by a widowed innkeeper, Kyssarra learned to listen before she learned to fight. She joined the Light late, and not for glory: she wanted a reason to keep walking the roads at night when no one else would.

She is kind to strangers and sharp with friends, and she keeps a list of every name she could not save.
