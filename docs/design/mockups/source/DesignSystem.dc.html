Spacing and shape
Spacing steps 4 · 8 · 12 · 16 · 20 · 28. Radius 6 for controls, 10 for panels, round for chips. Control heights: 30 compact · 36 standard · 44 navigation. Rail 272 wide; main area 772 at the 1100 × 720 window.
Buttons
One primary action per panel. Destructive actions sit apart and always ask first. Every control has a text label; an icon alone only for Close, Previous and Next.
